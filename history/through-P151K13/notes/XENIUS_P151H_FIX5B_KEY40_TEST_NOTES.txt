XENIUS P151H FIX5B — KEY40 IDEMPOTENT ACQUIRE TEST
Vivo X300 Ultra / Xenius LMC 8.3 · 2026-11-18
Package com.meitu.xenius135a01 · APK unsigned (sign with MT Manager)

PURPOSE
FIX5A removed getC2apiPref(null) from Llel.h and user reported shutter yielded no photo.
FIX5B restores the byte-for-byte working FIX4 classes.dex, including the request-time key40 call.
FIX5B makes the existing key40 helper treat repeated acquired-state calls as idempotent:
 in classes2.dex LMC/c2a.getC2apiPref at CU340
 existing if-nez v4 -> CU555 (increment gate counter and return)
 now if-nez v4 -> CU559 (return directly; NO count increment).
Exactly one non-header executable byte differs from FIX4 (branch delta 215 -> 219).
Original cold acquisition and single final restore path are unchanged.

WHY THIS MIGHT HELP
Request-time calls are now available again to activate key40 for the FullRes shot,
but they cannot inflate the gate counter beyond 1 once acquired.
Therefore a final device close can trigger the previous-value restore instead of leaving
key40=1 solely due to an unbalanced count.

NOT A COMPLETE LIFECYCLE REWRITE
- The alternate Larb path still releases immediately after CameraDevice.close() returns.
- Multiple overlapping camera-device opens are NOT safely reference counted by this patch;
  releasing one could restore while another is open. This must be solved before declaring final.
- The getInfo(40) readback, camera-idle safe recovery, and onClosed handling are not finished.
- Offline DEX checks do not prove Android ART acceptance or actual camera correctness.
- The gate is global; do not use stock camera simultaneously with Xenius.

WHAT IS UNCHANGED
- classes.dex is byte-identical to working P151H FIX4: RAW capture, HDR+, 2/3/5
  selection, automatic DNG cleanup, camera open/close logic and capture request call.
- All native libs and resources identical to FIX4.
- Same package name as original; unsigned for signing with MT Manager.
